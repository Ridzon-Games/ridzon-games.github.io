# Ridzon Games

Ridzon Games is the one-person studio of Daniel Ridzoň, Czech Republic.

## Tiny Observatory

Place a tiny observatory on your desk, and above it the real sky over your real location, right
now. Queue observations of real objects, build better telescopes, and learn why some of the
universe can only be seen from space.

- [Privacy Policy](tiny-observatory/privacy)
- [Terms & Conditions](tiny-observatory/terms)

## AR-TD

A tower-defense game played in augmented reality on your table, alone or with friends nearby.

- [Privacy Policy](ar-td/privacy)

## Support

Questions, bug reports and feedback: **support@ridzongames.com**

If you are a TestFlight tester, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app. To send your
save file, open the Files app, find it under *On My iPhone → Tiny Observatory*, and attach
`state.json` to an email. It contains the location you played from.
